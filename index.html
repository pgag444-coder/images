Our story

From passion to your table 📖

From a childhood kitchen to your table.

Made with love, served with joy.

It all began years ago with a young girl named Sveta Arakelyan and a simple, joyful love for baking. Spending countless hours in her family's kitchen, Sveta poured her heart into perfecting her cake recipes, creating sweet treats that brought smiles to everyone who tasted them. What started as a humble childhood passion has blossomed into the thriving business you see today. At AvNa & CheeseCake, we still hold true to Sveta’s original dedication to quality and love, ensuring every slice we serve feels like home.
